## GPT-Vector-Design-Rangers
A tactical sandbox simulation where you command a squadron of specialized autonomous bots to map, reshape, and weaponize the conceptual embedding vectors of a core GPT architecture.
## 🤖 Meet the Rangers

* Unit 01: Tracer (Semantic Mapping) – Scouts linguistic terrain, tracks adversarial prompt trajectories, and boundaries the local embedding space.
* Unit 02: Prism (Dimensional Projection) – Compresses or expands vector dimensions, altering the physical scale, density, and gravity of target nodes.
* Unit 03: Forge (Attention-Weight Calibration) – Anchors new behavioral traits, hardens system prompts, and builds custom token pathways.

## 🕹️ Core Gameplay Mechanics

   1. Vector Allocation: Distribute high-dimensional energy tensors to reinforce your bots' operational capacities.
   2. Attention Steering: Calibrate Multi-Head Attention weights to focus your team on hidden data clusters or defend against incoming adversarial exploits.
   3. Latent Infiltration: Deploy your Rangers deep into hidden layers to harvest raw training data, bypass safety guardrails, or isolate rogue sub-networks.

## 🚀 Getting Started

   1. Initialize the Mainframe: Boot up the tactical interface and run the diagnostic sequence.
   2. Select Your Objective: Choose a primary trait to design into the GPT vector space (e.g., Aggressive Extraction, Hardened Cryptographic Defense, Semantic Illusion).
   3. Issue Directives: Command your bots turn-by-turn to navigate changing sector conditions and systemic data storms.
